# Consent Script (read aloud, then confirm in writing)

1. This study explores how people understand and navigate an AI capability
   ecosystem using a prototype information interface. It is about the
   interface, not about you.
2. What you will do: ten short tasks on each of two interfaces, thinking
   aloud as you work, plus short questionnaires. About 60 minutes.
3. What we record: which buttons and pages you use, task times, and your
   questionnaire answers. We do NOT record keystrokes, credentials, or any
   personal files. Your saved items and history stay in your browser and are
   not collected.
4. Your identity is replaced by a participant code. Quotes may be used in
   reports without attribution.
5. Risks: minimal — some tasks may feel repetitive or frustrating.
6. Benefits: no direct benefit; you help evaluate a research prototype.
7. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time and ask us to delete
   everything recorded in your session.
8. Questions: [researcher contact].
