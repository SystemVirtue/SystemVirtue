# Interview Guide

## Think-aloud instructions (start of session)

"As you work, please say out loud what you are looking at, what you are
trying to do, and what you expect to happen. There are no wrong answers; we
are evaluating the interface, not you."

Neutral prompts only: "What are you thinking now?" / "What did you expect
to see?"

## Post-task probes (every task)

1. "Where are you right now in the ecosystem, and why is it relevant?"
   (context retention — score 0/0.5/1 against ground truth)
2. "How confident are you that what you found fits the task?" (0–100)
3. For clicks: "What did you expect to be behind that link?" (information
   scent — record predicted vs actual)

## End-of-condition questions

- Which single feature helped you most? Which got in the way?
- Could you tell how trustworthy the information was? How?
- Did you feel lost at any point? Where?

## End-of-session questions (both conditions done)

1. Forced preference: "If you had to do tomorrow's work in one of the two
   interfaces, which one, and why?"
2. Mental-map probe: "Sketch or describe how the things you saw relate to
   each other." (score structure against the corpus graph; feeds recall)
3. Uncertainty probe: "What was the system least sure about?"
4. Recommendation why-test: "Why do you think it suggested that?"
5. What would you want next that neither interface offered?

## Recording

Notes + screen recording where consented. Map every observation to a task id
so it can be triangulated with the event log.
